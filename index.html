How we work

A delivery rhythm you can plan around

Fixed milestones, working software every two weeks, and no surprises at handover.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Workshops with your team to map processes, systems, users and constraints.

  2. 2

    Design

    Architecture, integration map and clickable UI — approved before a line of code.

  3. 3

    Build

    Two-week sprints with demos, code review and automated tests as standard.

  4. 4

    Integrate

    Connect to payments, ERP and government services in a controlled test environment.

  5. 5

    Launch

    UAT, security review, training and a rollout plan with a rollback path.

  6. 6

    Support

    Monitoring, SLA response and a roadmap for the next release.
